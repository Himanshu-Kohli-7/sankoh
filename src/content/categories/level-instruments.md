faqs:
  - question: What is the difference between a level transmitter and a level switch?
    answer: A transmitter measures continuously and sends a signal that varies with level. A switch only detects whether the level has reached one point and changes a contact. Transmitters suit monitoring and control; switches suit alarms and simple pump control.
  - question: Which level instrument works without touching the liquid?
    answer: Radar and ultrasonic transmitters. Both are mounted on top of the tank and time echoes from the surface, so nothing is wetted except, at most, the antenna or sensor face exposed to vapour.
  - question: Can a pressure transmitter measure level?
    answer: Yes. On an open tank, a deeper column of liquid presses harder on the base, so bottom pressure gives the level directly. On a closed or pressurised tank, a differential pressure transmitter compares the bottom with the space above the liquid.
  - question: What should I use for a cement or grain silo?
    answer: For high and low alarms, a rotary paddle or vibrating fork switch made for solids. For a continuous reading of how full the silo is, a radar transmitter is the usual choice because dust does not trouble it.

Level instruments tell you how full a tank, sump or silo is, or warn you when it reaches a limit. They prevent overflows and dry-running pumps, keep stock records honest and let a control system fill and empty vessels on its own.

Transmitters give a continuous reading. A [radar level transmitter](/products/level-instruments/radar-level-transmitter/) or an [ultrasonic level transmitter](/products/level-instruments/ultrasonic-level-transmitter/) measures from above without touching the contents. A [capacitance level transmitter](/products/level-instruments/capacitance-level-transmitter/) uses a probe inside the tank, and a [side mounted level transmitter](/products/level-instruments/side-mounted-level-transmitter/) follows the liquid with a float. A [pressure transmitter](/products/pressure-instruments/pressure-transmitter/) or [differential pressure transmitter](/products/pressure-instruments/differential-pressure-transmitter/) can also read level from the weight of liquid.

Switches act at one point: a [float type level switch](/products/level-instruments/float-type-level-switch/) for clean liquids, a [vibrating fork level switch](/products/level-instruments/vibrating-fork-level-switch/) that handles both liquids and powders, and a [rotary paddle level switch](/products/level-instruments/rotary-paddle-level-switch/) for bulk solids.
