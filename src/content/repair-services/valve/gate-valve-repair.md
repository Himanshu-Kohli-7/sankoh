Gate valves often sit in one position for months and then refuse to move when they are needed. A repair starts with freeing and stripping the valve. The gate, seats, stem, stem nut and packing are cleaned and examined, and worn or damaged parts are repaired or replaced.

Knife gate valves get the same treatment, with extra attention to the blade and the seals that wear against it, since slurry is hard on both. On motorised gate valves the actuator is checked along with the valve.

Each gate valve is tested before it is dispatched, and you receive a report of the work. A valve whose body is cracked is usually better replaced, and we supply new [gate valves](/products/manual-valves/gate-valve/) as well.
