In most butterfly valves the seal is a rubber or plastic seat lining the body, with the disc closing against it. That seat is the part that wears, so a repair usually centres on it. The valve is dismantled, the disc, shaft, bushes and seals are examined, and the seat and any other worn parts are repaired or replaced.

Actuated valves have their actuator checked too, including the coupling and its alignment with the disc, since a small misalignment can stop the disc closing fully. Spherical disc and other designs are handled to suit the way each one seals.

Each repaired valve is tested before dispatch, and we send a report of the work with it. Where the body is corroded through or the disc is beyond repair, we can quote for a new [butterfly valve](/products/manual-valves/butterfly-valve/).
