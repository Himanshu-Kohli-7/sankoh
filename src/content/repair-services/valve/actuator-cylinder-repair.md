Pneumatic actuators and cylinders are opened up, cleaned and examined. Seals that have hardened or worn are renewed, and the pistons, bore, shaft and any return springs are checked for wear and corrosion. Electric actuators are worked through motor, gearbox, wiring and controls to find what has stopped them.

When the actuator comes in on its valve, we also look at the coupling and mounting between the two, because play there can make a sound actuator seem faulty. A positioner fitted to the actuator can be repaired and calibrated in the same job.

After the work, the actuator is tested before it goes back to you, along with a report of what was done. If the valve underneath needs work as well, see [ball valve repair](/valve-repair/ball-valve-repair/) or [butterfly valve repair](/valve-repair/butterfly-valve-repair/).
