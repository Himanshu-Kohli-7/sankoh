A positioner reads the control signal, checks it against the valve's real position, and raises or lowers the air to the actuator to close the gap. When it goes wrong, the cause can be inside the positioner, in the linkage that feeds back the valve's position, or in the air supply to it.

We check each of these, repair or replace the faulty parts, and then calibrate the positioner to your control signal, so the valve opening follows the signal from one end of its range to the other. A positioner gives its best results when calibrated on the valve it controls, so send the assembly if you can.

The positioner is tested before dispatch, and a report of the work comes back with it. If the actuator or valve also needs attention, see [actuator & cylinder repair](/valve-repair/actuator-cylinder-repair/) and [control valve repair](/valve-repair/control-valve-repair/).
