A plug valve seals across the large tapered or cylindrical face of its plug, so a little damage or deposit there makes it stiff or lets it pass. A repair means taking the plug out, cleaning and examining the plug and body, and repairing or replacing the sealing parts, stem seals and any other worn components.

Plug valves are often actuated because of the force they need to turn, so the actuator on a pneumatic or motorised plug valve is checked at the same time. A plug that has become stiff can overload an otherwise healthy actuator.

The valve is tested before dispatch and returned with a report of what was done. See [actuator & cylinder repair](/valve-repair/actuator-cylinder-repair/) if only the actuator needs attention.
