A servo drive controls a servo motor's position, speed and torque, using feedback from an encoder on the motor. Because the drive, the motor and the cable between them work as one system, a fault that looks like a drive problem can sometimes begin elsewhere.

We inspect the drive, trace the fault and repair or replace the faulty parts. It helps to know the alarm code, the motor model and whether the fault appeared suddenly or got worse over time. If the source of the fault is unclear, say so, and we will advise on what to send.

Before dispatch, every servo drive is tested, and the report of the work travels with it.
