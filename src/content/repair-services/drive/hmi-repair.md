An HMI is the operator's window into the machine, so a failed panel can stop production even when everything behind it is working. Most faults lie in the display and its backlight, the touch panel, the power supply or the communication ports.

At our workshop we open the panel, find the fault and repair or replace whatever has failed. If you have a backup of the HMI's project or program, keep it safe and mention it. Whether it is needed depends on the fault, but it is worth having in case the panel's memory is affected.

Each HMI is tested before dispatch and comes back with a report of the work done.
