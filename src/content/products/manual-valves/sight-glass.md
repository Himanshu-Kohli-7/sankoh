A sight glass, also called a sight flow indicator, is a fitting with a glass tube or window that lets you look inside a pipeline. It answers questions that no gauge can. Is liquid really flowing? How clear is it, and what colour?

It has nothing to operate. Fluid passes through the body and is seen through the glass. Some designs add a flap, ball or spinner that moves with the flow, which makes even a clear liquid easy to see.

Sight glasses are made with flanged, screwed or hygienic clamp ends to suit the line. The glass is the most fragile part of the pipeline, so the pressure, temperature and media have to be known before one is chosen. A common use is after a [steam trap](/products/manual-valves/steam-traps/), to show that it is discharging condensate.
