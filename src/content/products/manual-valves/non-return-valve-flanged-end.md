  - question: What is the difference between a swing check and a lift check valve?
    answer: A swing check valve has a hinged disc that swings clear of the flow. It gives a low pressure drop and suits liquids at steady flow. A non-return valve, also called a check valve or NRV, lets fluid flow in one direction and closes on its own when the flow tries to reverse. It needs no handle, air or power. The flow pushes the disc off its seat. When the flow stops, the weight of the disc and the back pressure close it again.

Its job is protection. It stops a pump being driven backwards when it trips, keeps a line from draining back and prevents one stream running into another.

Flanged check valves are most often the swing type, with a hinged disc under a bolted cover, or the lift type, with a guided disc that rises off the seat. The cover gives access for inspection, and an arrow on the body shows the direction of flow.
