Forged valves are small gate, globe and check valves whose bodies are forged from solid metal instead of being cast. Forging presses the hot metal into shape under high force, which gives a dense, strong body. That strength lets the valve stay compact while handling demanding pressures and temperatures.

They work in the same way as their larger cast versions. A forged [gate valve](/products/manual-valves/gate-valve/) isolates, a forged [globe valve](/products/manual-valves/globe-valve/) regulates and a forged check valve stops reverse flow. What sets them apart is their build. They have short, heavy-walled bodies for small-bore pipework, most often with screwed or socket weld ends.

You will find them on drains, vents, bypasses and instrument connections, and wherever a small line carries steam or another high-pressure fluid.
