A screwed-end ball valve is a quarter-turn shut-off valve with threaded ends. It screws straight onto threaded pipe, with no flanges, gaskets or bolts. Inside, a ball with a hole through it turns between two seats. With the lever along the pipe, the valve is open. With the lever across it, the valve is shut.

Threaded ends make the valve small, light and quick to fit, so it is the everyday isolation valve on small-bore lines. Bodies are made in one, two or three pieces. The three-piece design lets the centre section be taken out for service while the ends stay in the line.

For larger lines, or where the valve must be unbolted often, see the [flanged-end ball valve](/products/manual-valves/ball-valve-flanged-end/).
