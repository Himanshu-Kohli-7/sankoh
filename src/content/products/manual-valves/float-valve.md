faqs:
  - question: How is a float valve different from a float switch?
    answer: A float valve controls the water itself. Its float works the valve mechanically, with no wiring. A float switch only makes or breaks an electrical contact, which then starts or stops a pump or works a solenoid valve.
  - question: Can I change the level at which the tank stops filling?
    answer: Usually yes. Bending or adjusting the float arm, or moving the float along it, sets the shut-off level. Leave enough gap below the overflow so a slow drip from a worn seat does not run to waste.
  - question: Why does my float valve hum or hammer?
    answer: The float is bobbing on ripples near the shut-off point, opening and closing the valve rapidly. A damping chamber, a larger float or a calmer spot in the tank usually cures it. High supply pressure can make it worse.
  - question: Will it work on low or high supply pressure?
    answer: The orifice has to match the supply. A large orifice gives fast filling on a low-pressure supply but may not seal against high pressure. A small one seals well at high pressure but fills slowly. Tell us the supply pressure and the flow you need.

A float valve, often called a ball float valve or ballcock, keeps a tank topped up without anyone touching it. It sits on the inlet pipe, and a buoyant float on an arm rests on the water. As the level falls the float drops, the arm swings and the valve opens. As the tank fills the float lifts and pushes a washer onto the seat until the flow stops at the set height.

Small valves work directly through the lever. On large inlets the force needed to close against the supply grows too big for a float, so a pilot design is used. The float works a small pilot, and the supply pressure itself closes the main valve.

The valve needs no power and has few parts: a seat, a washer and a pivot. Fit an isolating valve ahead of it for servicing. Where a pump or alarm has to react to the level instead, use a [float type level switch](/products/level-instruments/float-type-level-switch/).
