faqs:
  - question: How do I set the pump-on and pump-off levels?
    answer: By the free cable length between the float and the counterweight or clamp. The float tips one way at the top of its swing and the other way at the bottom, so a longer free length gives a wider gap between start and stop. Two separate floats give two independent levels.
  - question: Does it matter whether the pump fills or empties the tank?
    answer: Yes. The same float can either close its contact when it rises, to empty a sump, or close when it falls, to fill a tank. Changeover (SPDT) floats do either, depending on which wires you use.
  - question: How is it different from a stem-mounted float switch?
    answer: A stem type slides a float along a fixed rod through a short distance, so it needs a tank-top fitting and clean liquid. A cable float hangs freely, needs no fitting, gives a wide differential and copes with dirty water and sewage where a stem float would jam.
  - question: Can it run the pump motor directly?
    answer: Power-rated cable floats can switch a small single-phase pump within their contact rating. For larger or three-phase pumps, the float works the starter or a control relay.

A cable float switch is a small, sealed float hanging on its own electrical cable. A weight clamped to the cable, or a bracket, sets the pivot point. While the float hangs down in air, the switch inside it rests in one position. As the liquid rises, the float lifts and swings upward. Past a certain angle, a ball or weight inside rolls over and operates a microswitch.

The movement is a full tilt rather than a slide along a stem, so the gap between switch-on and switch-off depends on how much cable is left free. It can be set wide, which stops a pump from starting and stopping too often.

With nothing fixed to the tank, it can be lowered into a pit, well or sump in minutes, and solids and sewage do not stop it working. For clean tanks with a top fitting, a [float type level switch](/products/level-instruments/float-type-level-switch/) gives more precise switching points. For a continuous level reading, use a [hydrostatic level transmitter](/products/level-instruments/hydrostatic-level-transmitter/).
