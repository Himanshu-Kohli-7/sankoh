faqs:
  - question: How does a magnetic level indicator work?
    answer: A chamber is connected to the side of the tank so the liquid in it stands at the same level as in the tank. A float with a magnet inside rides on that liquid. As it passes, the magnet turns over a row of small flaps or rollers on the outside of the chamber, usually white above the level and red below.
  - question: Why choose it over a gauge glass?
    answer: The liquid stays inside a welded metal chamber, so there is no glass to break or leak and nothing to stain. The red and white column is easy to read from a distance, and the indicator copes with higher pressures and more hazardous liquids.
  - question: What must be known to size the float?
    answer: The liquid's density, or specific gravity, and the working pressure and temperature. The float is built to sit correctly in that liquid and to withstand that pressure, so a float made for water will read wrongly in a lighter liquid.
  - question: Can it give alarms or a remote signal?
    answer: Yes. Magnetic switches clamped to the chamber give high and low alarms at any height, and a transmitter strip beside the chamber gives a 4–20 mA signal. Both work from the same float.

A magnetic level indicator shows the level in a tank on a tall, brightly coloured scale, without the liquid ever touching glass. It is made of three parts: a chamber piped to the side of the tank, a magnetic float inside that chamber, and an indicator rail fixed to the outside.

The chamber forms a second, narrow column of the tank's liquid. The float follows the surface up and down, and its magnetic field reaches through the chamber wall to turn each flap on the rail as it goes by. The flaps keep their position with no power, so the reading remains even in a blackout.

Since the wetted parts are a closed metal or plastic tube, the instrument suits pressure vessels and liquids you would not want behind glass. It also needs very little upkeep: there is no glass to clean and no gland to tighten. For clean, low-hazard liquids where a simple direct view is enough, a [level gauge glass](/products/level-instruments/level-gauge-glass/) costs less.
