faqs:
  - question: Single acting or double acting cylinder?
    answer: Double acting is the usual choice. It gives full force in both directions, which a gate cutting through settled solids needs. Single acting, with a spring, is chosen where a lost air supply has to leave the gate shut, or fully open, and it needs a larger cylinder to overcome the spring.
  - question: What accessories are fitted?
    answer: Normally a solenoid valve to switch the air, a filter regulator on the supply, and limit switches or a switch box to confirm open and closed. A manual handwheel override can be added for use when there is no air.
  - question: Can it throttle the flow?
    answer: It is meant for on/off service. Held part-open, the solids wear the gate edge and the seat quickly. If you need to control the flow of a slurry, tell us the duty and we will suggest a suitable valve.
  - question: Will it handle very thick sludge?
    answer: The thin gate cuts through most sludges and fibrous stock. Very heavy or sticky material may need a bigger cylinder, a scraper in the packing, or a flushing connection, so tell us what the line carries.

A pneumatic knife gate valve is a [knife gate valve](/products/manual-valves/knife-gate-valve/) with a compressed-air cylinder mounted on its yoke. The cylinder rod is coupled to the top of the thin gate. When the solenoid valve sends air to one side of the piston, the rod drives the gate down through the line, and its sharp lower edge cuts through solids, fibres and settled sludge to seal. Air on the other side lifts it clear again.

The cylinder gives a fast, forceful stroke that a person on a handwheel cannot match, and it lets the valve be controlled remotely. That is useful on large lines, on valves in awkward places, and wherever the valve has to shut as part of a sequence.

A typical assembly carries a [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/), an air filter regulator and position switches. On request it also has a handwheel for manual operation. For the cylinder on its own, see the [pneumatic cylinder](/products/valve-automation-accessories/pneumatic-cylinder/).
