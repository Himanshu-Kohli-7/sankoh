A pneumatic tri-clover ball valve is a ball valve built for hygienic service, with tri-clover (TC) clamp ends instead of flanges or threads, turned by an air-powered actuator rather than a handle. It opens and closes product and cleaning lines automatically, on a signal from the plant's control system.

The ball, with a bore through it, turns a quarter turn between soft seats. When open, the bore lines up with the pipe to give a smooth, full passage that is easy to flush. The clamp ends mean the valve can be lifted out for inspection or cleaning, then clamped back without disturbing the pipework.

Air to the actuator is switched by a [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/), here mounted directly on the actuator. A [limit switch box](/products/valve-automation-accessories/) above it tells the panel whether the line is open or shut, which matters when product and cleaning fluids must never mix.
