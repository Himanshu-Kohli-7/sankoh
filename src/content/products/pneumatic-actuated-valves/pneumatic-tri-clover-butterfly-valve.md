A pneumatic tri-clover butterfly valve is a hygienic butterfly valve with tri-clover (TC) clamp ends, driven by a compressed-air actuator. It gives automatic open/close control on sanitary pipework where the line must stay clean, drain fully and come apart easily.

The body is made in two halves that hold a moulded rubber seat between them. A disc on a short shaft turns a quarter turn inside the seat: edge-on to open, pressed into the rubber to close. With no pockets for product to collect in, the valve is straightforward to clean in place, and its short body makes it practical on larger hygienic lines.

The actuator mounts on a bracket above the valve. A [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/) on the actuator switches it, and a [limit switch box](/products/valve-automation-accessories/) confirms the disc position to the PLC.
