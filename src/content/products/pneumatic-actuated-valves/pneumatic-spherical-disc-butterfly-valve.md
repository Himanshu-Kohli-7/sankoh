A pneumatic spherical disc butterfly valve is a quarter-turn butterfly valve with two differences from the ordinary kind: the edge of its disc is shaped to part of a sphere, and it is opened and closed by a compressed-air actuator rather than by hand.

In a standard butterfly valve, the flat disc edge wedges into a soft seat, which wears with use. A spherical edge rolls into contact with the seat evenly around its whole circumference. The result is a closer, more consistent seal, less rubbing as the valve cycles, and a valve that keeps the short, light body of a butterfly design.

The actuator turns the disc through 90 degrees. A [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/) switches the air on command, and a [limit switch box or positioner](/products/valve-automation-accessories/) can be added for position feedback or modulating duty.
