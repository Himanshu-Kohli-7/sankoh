faqs:
  - question: How does a plug valve differ from a ball valve?
    answer: Both turn a quarter turn, but a plug valve seals over a much larger area, along the whole face of the plug. That broad contact wipes the sealing surfaces as the valve turns, which helps with dirty or sticky media. A ball valve usually needs less torque to operate.
  - question: What are sleeved and lubricated plug valves?
    answer: In a sleeved valve, the plug turns inside a replaceable plastic sleeve that lines the body and makes the seal. In a lubricated valve, a sealing compound is injected between the plug and body from time to time. The right type depends on the media and how often the valve operates.
  - question: Can one plug valve send flow to different lines?
    answer: Yes. Multi-port plug valves have three or four connections, and the passage through the plug is shaped to link different pairs of ports as it turns. One actuated valve can then replace two or more on/off valves.
  - question: Why does a plug valve need a larger actuator?
    answer: The friction between plug and sleeve or body is higher than in a ball or butterfly valve of the same size, so it takes more torque to turn. We size the actuator on the actual valve and your air pressure, with a margin for media that may stick.

A pneumatic plug valve is a quarter-turn valve in which a tapered or cylindrical plug, with a passage cut through it, turns inside the body to open or close the line. A compressed-air actuator on top turns the plug through 90 degrees on a signal from the panel or PLC.

When the passage lines up with the pipe, the media flows straight through. Turned across, the solid side of the plug closes off the ports. Because the plug presses against the body or a sleeve over a broad area, the seal is tolerant of dirty, viscous or sticky media that would damage the seat of a lighter valve.

The higher friction of the plug means the actuator is sized with more margin than for a ball valve. A [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/) switches the air, and [limit switches](/products/valve-automation-accessories/) report the plug position back to the control system.
