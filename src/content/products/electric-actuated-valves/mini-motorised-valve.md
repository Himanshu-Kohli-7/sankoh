A mini motorised valve pairs a small ball valve with a compact electric actuator about the size of a hand. A low-voltage motor and gear train turn the ball a quarter turn to open or close the line. Limit switches inside stop the motor at each end. The actuator clips or screws onto the valve neck, and most take power straight from a controller, timer or relay.

It fills the gap between a solenoid valve and a full-size [electric actuated ball valve](/products/electric-actuated-valves/electric-actuated-ball-valve/). It is small and cheap enough for appliances and dispensers. Unlike a solenoid valve, it gives a full bore, needs no minimum line pressure and holds position without drawing power.

That is why it is common in water ATMs, RO purifiers and HVAC fan coil units, where many valves switch clean water at low pressure. Where the line is tiny and switching must be instant, a [direct acting solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/direct-acting-solenoid-valve/) is still the better fit.
