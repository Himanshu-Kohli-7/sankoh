faqs:
  - question: Can an electric butterfly valve hold a part-open position?
    answer: Yes, if it has a modulating actuator. That type accepts an analogue signal from the controller, drives the disc to the matching angle and sends its actual position back. An on/off actuator only stops at its two end positions.
  - question: Why does the actuator need a margin over the valve torque?
    answer: The disc needs its greatest effort at the first moment of opening, when it has to break free of the rubber seat with full line pressure behind it. A seat that has dried out or a line that has stood shut for months makes that worse, so the actuator is chosen with spare torque.
  - question: Can it be installed outdoors?
    answer: Yes, with a weatherproof actuator, sealed cable glands and the conduit entries facing down so rain cannot run in. Many actuators carry a small internal heater that stops condensation forming inside the housing. Let us know if the valve will stand in the open.

An electric actuated wafer butterfly valve pairs a short, lightweight butterfly valve with an electric motor drive. It is the usual way to motorise isolation on big-bore water and air lines, because the valve body adds little length or weight to the pipework even at large sizes.

The valve body is a narrow ring clamped between two pipe flanges. Through its middle runs a shaft carrying a round disc. The actuator rotates the shaft through 90 degrees: turned edge-on, the disc lets the flow pass, and turned square to the pipe, it presses into the rubber lining of the body and seals.

Electric actuators move the disc steadily rather than snapping it shut, which helps limit surge in long water mains. Their worm gearing also holds the disc firmly at any angle, so a modulating version can throttle or balance a circuit as well as isolate it. The handwheel on the side is for manual operation without power. Position feedback and local controls are covered under [valve automation accessories](/products/valve-automation-accessories/), and air-driven valves switched by a [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/) are an alternative where instrument air is to hand.
