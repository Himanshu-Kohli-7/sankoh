An electric tri-clover butterfly valve is a sanitary butterfly valve joined to the pipe by tri-clover (TC) clamps and moved by an electric actuator instead of a hand lever. It suits dairy, food and pharmaceutical plants that want automatic valves but have no instrument air, and larger sanitary lines where a ball valve would be bulky.

The valve body splits into two stainless halves that grip a moulded rubber seat. A disc on a short shaft sits in the seat. A quarter turn sets it edge-on for flow, and a quarter turn back presses its rim into the rubber to close the line. There are no cavities around the disc, so product drains away and cleaning solution reaches every wetted surface.

The actuator sits high on an extended stem and turns the disc through a reduction gear, stopping at fully open and fully closed. A manual handwheel and a dome position indicator are built in. Position feedback for the PLC is covered under [valve automation accessories](/products/valve-automation-accessories/). If compressed air is available, air-operated valves switched by [solenoid valves](/products/solenoid-valves/) cycle faster.
