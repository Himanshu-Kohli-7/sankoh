  - question: Why is the actuator mounted on a long stem?

An electric tri-clover butterfly valve is a sanitary butterfly valve joined to the pipe by tri-clover (TC) clamps and moved by an electric actuator instead of a hand lever. It suits dairy, food and pharma plants without instrument air, and larger sanitary lines.

The body splits into two stainless halves that grip a moulded rubber seat. Its disc swings 90 degrees on a stub shaft, edge-on for flow and bedded into the rubber to shut. Having no cavities around the disc, product drains away and cleaning solution reaches every wetted surface.

The actuator sits high on an extended stem and turns the disc through a reduction gear, stopping at fully open and fully closed. A handwheel and dome indicator are built in. PLC feedback is covered under [valve automation accessories](/products/valve-automation-accessories/). With compressed air available, air-operated valves switched by [solenoid valves](/products/solenoid-valves/) cycle faster.
