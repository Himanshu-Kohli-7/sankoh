An electric UPVC ball valve is a plastic ball valve made from unplasticised PVC, motorised so it opens and shuts on an electrical command. It is a common choice for RO plants, water ATMs and chemical dosing systems, which are built from plastic pipe, carry water or chemicals that would corrode steel, and often have no compressed air at all.

The ball inside is drilled straight through and rotates a quarter turn between two seats. In line with the pipe, it lets water through; turned across, it shuts the line tight. The body is a true-union design. Two large nuts hold it between end pieces fixed to the pipe, so loosening them frees the whole valve and actuator without cutting or unscrewing any pipework. The valve shown has threaded ends.

The actuator is held above the body by a bracket and coupling. A level switch, timer or PLC can command it, and a handwheel moves it if power is lost. The dome indicator shows its position. Feedback and local control options are listed under [valve automation accessories](/products/valve-automation-accessories/). Plastic-bodied [solenoid valves](/products/solenoid-valves/) are a lighter, cheaper option for small, frequently switched lines.
