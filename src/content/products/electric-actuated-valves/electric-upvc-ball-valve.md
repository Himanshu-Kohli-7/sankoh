  - question: Can the actuator weight damage a plastic valve or pipe?

An electric UPVC ball valve is a ball valve moulded from unplasticised PVC and motorised to open and shut on an electrical command. RO plants, water ATMs and dosing systems use it widely, as they run on plastic pipe, carry corrosive liquids and often lack compressed air.

The ball inside is drilled straight through and rotates a quarter turn between two seats. The body is a true-union design, held by two large nuts between end pieces fixed to the pipe. Loosen them and valve and actuator lift out without cutting pipework. The valve shown has threaded ends.

A bracket and coupling hold the actuator above the body. A level switch, timer or PLC can command it, a handwheel moves it without power, and a dome shows its position. Options are listed under [valve automation accessories](/products/valve-automation-accessories/). Plastic [solenoid valves](/products/solenoid-valves/) are a lighter choice for small, frequently switched lines.
