A motorised globe valve is a globe valve with an electric actuator that drives its stem up and down, so it can hold any position between shut and fully open. It is the electric counterpart of an air-operated control valve, widely used for heating and cooling water in buildings without compressed air.

Flow passes through an opening in the body called the seat. A plug on the stem moves towards or away from it, and the gap sets how much passes. Moving along the flow rather than swinging across it, the plug gives fine, stable regulation over most of its travel.

The actuator turns motor rotation into a straight push and pull on the stem. The actuator positions the valve to match a signal from a controller, thermostat or BMS. Most have a manual knob. [Valve automation accessories](/products/valve-automation-accessories/) add position signals. Small on/off duties may suit a [solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/).
