faqs:
  - question: Why automate a diaphragm valve electrically rather than with air?
    answer: Many water treatment and chemical sites have power at every valve but no reliable compressed air. An electric actuator avoids running an air network, and with a positioner card it can hold the valve at part opening for flow control.
  - question: What kind of actuator drives it?
    answer: A linear or multi-turn electric actuator that pushes the compressor down onto the diaphragm, fitted with torque or travel limits so it closes firmly without crushing the diaphragm. A handwheel override is common.
  - question: Is the diaphragm a wear part?
    answer: Yes. Its life depends on the chemical, the temperature and how often the valve cycles, so on a frequently cycled valve it is worth keeping a spare. The diaphragm can be changed without removing the body from the line.
  - question: What happens if power fails?
    answer: A standard actuator simply stops in its last position. For a valve that has to shut, or open, on loss of power, ask for a battery or spring fail-safe unit.

An electric diaphragm valve is a [diaphragm valve](/products/manual-valves/diaphragm-valve/) driven by a motorised actuator rather than turned by hand. When the actuator runs, it drives the compressor down and presses the flexible diaphragm onto the weir or the body floor to shut the line. Reversing it lifts the diaphragm clear and the full passage opens again.

The design keeps the fluid away from every moving part. Only the lining and the diaphragm are wetted, so the actuator and stem stay dry and there is no gland packing to leak. That makes it a natural choice for corrosive chemicals, abrasive slurries and hygienic liquids, the same duties as the manual valve.

The actuator is sized for the diaphragm's closing force at your line pressure and can carry limit switches and position feedback. For the actuator alone, see the [electric actuator](/products/valve-automation-accessories/electric-actuator/). For small automated chemical lines, an [electric UPVC ball valve](/products/electric-actuated-valves/electric-upvc-ball-valve/) is the simpler option.
