faqs:
  - question: 3/2 way or 5/2 way?
    answer: Match it to the actuator. A spring return actuator needs a 3/2 way valve, which fills the actuator to move it and vents it to let the springs return. A double acting actuator needs a 5/2 way valve, which pressurises one side while venting the other. Many NAMUR valves convert between the two with a reversible plate.
  - question: What happens when the power fails?
    answer: A single-coil valve springs back and the actuator returns to its de-energised position, which is how fail-safe valves are arranged. A double-coil, bistable valve stays in its last position until the other coil is energised.
  - question: Will it fit my actuator?
    answer: If the actuator has the standard NAMUR interface on its side, the valve bolts straight on with the supplied screws and seals. Check the actuator's air connection size, the coil voltage and the enclosure needed for the site.
  - question: Can I adjust how fast the valve opens and closes?
    answer: Yes. Small speed control silencers or flow regulators in the exhaust ports slow the actuator in either direction, which helps on valves that would otherwise slam shut.

A NAMUR solenoid valve is built for one job: switching the air to a quarter-turn pneumatic actuator. Its body has a flat face with two air ports and a fixed bolt pattern that matches the NAMUR interface found on the side of most rack and pinion and scotch yoke actuators. It bolts straight on, so there are no pipes or fittings between valve and actuator to leak, kink or be knocked off.

Inside, a small pilot worked by the coil shifts a spool. In one position the spool sends supply air to the actuator; in the other it vents it, or, on a 5/2 way valve, swaps which side of the pistons gets the air. A manual override lets the valve be operated by hand during commissioning.

Choose the coil voltage and enclosure for the site, and feed the valve from an [air filter regulator](/products/valve-automation-accessories/air-filter-regulator/). For general-purpose valves that switch liquids or gases in a pipeline, see the [direct acting solenoid valve](/products/solenoid-valves/direct-acting-solenoid-valve/).
