Most of what a plant needs is on this site, but not everything. Lines built years ago, special duties and imported equipment often call for an item we do not list, and chasing it across several suppliers takes time your team may not have.

Send the details to us instead. We check our own range first, then search our vendor network for the exact item or a close equivalent, and give you the options in one reply. You can add listed products to the same enquiry, so a whole requirement is handled in one place.

If the item turns out to be discontinued, our [surplus & discontinued valves](/services/surplus-discontinued-valves/) service may still find it. If the answer is to keep the existing unit running, we can [repair it](/valve-repair/).
