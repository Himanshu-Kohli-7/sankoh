Good valve selection starts with the duty rather than the catalogue. The same line size can call for a butterfly valve on cooling water, a globe valve where flow must be throttled finely, or a ball valve where tight shut-off and quick operation matter most. Media that carry solids, steam or chemicals each narrow the choice of body and seat materials, and the way the valve is operated depends on how often it moves and whether it is part of an automated loop.

We work through these points with you, explain the trade-offs in plain terms, so your team can order with confidence. For valves already installed, we look at how they are used and what has gone wrong before, which usually points to the cause of repeat failures.

If the answer is a new valve, we supply it or source it to your specification. If a repair makes more sense, our [valve repair](/valve-repair/) team can take it on.
