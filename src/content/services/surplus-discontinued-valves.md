Valves often outlast the models they were sold as. A manufacturer updates a range or stops making a size, and years later a plant needs a spare seat, a stem or a whole valve that will fit the same space in the line. Changing the pipework to suit a different valve is costly, so a direct match is usually worth looking for first.

We start by identifying exactly what is installed from the nameplate, the photos and the dimensions. From there we look for surplus stock of the same model, discontinued parts, or reconditioned components, and we check whether the existing valve could simply be repaired instead.

Where none of these work, we suggest a current valve that suits the duty and fits the space, or [source one](/services/product-sourcing/) to your specification. For help deciding, see our [valve selection & maintenance advisory](/services/valve-selection-maintenance-advisory/).
